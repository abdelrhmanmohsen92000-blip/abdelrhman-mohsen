BIM & documentation

How I model, coordinate and document buildings, shown with drawings from real project packages.

View the project

From model to issued drawings

  1. 01

    Model

    Architectural models to LOD 350+ with parametric families, set up for worksharing and for the drawings they will produce.

  2. 02

    Federate

    Discipline models exported and appended in Navisworks into one federated model.

  3. 03

    Test

    Hard and soft clash tests between architecture, structure and MEP, filtered by discipline.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    Clashes logged, assigned, discussed in coordination meetings and fixed in the model, then re-tested until the report is clear.

  5. 05

    Document

    Plans, sections, details, schedules and BOQs issued from the coordinated model, through a CDE to ISO 19650.

What I deliver

Modelling

  • Architectural models to LOD 350+
  • Parametric families and assemblies
  • Exploded and cutaway axonometrics
  • IFC export

Coordination

  • Federated Arc/Str/MEP models in Navisworks
  • Clash tests, reports and issue logs
  • BCF issue exchange
  • 4D sequencing with TimeLiner

Documentation

  • Plans, elevations and sections
  • Wall sections and facade details
  • Shop drawings
  • Municipal licensing and tender packages

Data

  • Room finish schedules
  • Door and window schedules
  • Material take-offs and automated BOQ
  • Area schedules

Information management

  • CDE set-up on BIM 360 / ACC
  • WIP, shared, published and archived states
  • ISO 19650 naming and status codes
  • BIM execution planning
